About Me

I earned my B.A. in Geography from the University of Washington with a focus on GIS and data science. I then spent three years as a LiDAR analyst at a surveying firm in Bellevue, working with large geospatial datasets for utility infrastructure projects. Much of that work centered on data quality, and I helped improve our QA/QC process by standardizing reviews, automating repetitive tasks, and catching errors earlier.

I am now pursuing an M.S. in Information Systems at the University of Washington Foster School of Business, where I am building skills in SQL, Python, cloud technologies, BI tools, and AI. I am interested in data analytics, BI engineering, and data engineering roles, particularly in the technology industry. I am focusing my search on the Seattle area, the San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles and Orange County, and New York City.
